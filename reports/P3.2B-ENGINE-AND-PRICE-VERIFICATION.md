## 2. Engine correction (red first)

`PricingUnit.PerKilogram` (variant A03 may be priced per kilogram; the domain forces quantity unit `Kilogram`):

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `IsOrderLevelTotalComplete` | `true` although no amount can be derived | `false` |
| `RequestedQuantityTotal` | `rate x Quantity` whenever a selection carried a quantity (also on a rejected selection) | always `null` |
| reason | none | `ChargeableWeightNotDetermined` |

Why: no approved document defines the chargeable weight of a per-kilogram product (measured weight, weight above the allowance, weight above the bracket floor, or a stated kilogram quantity). The pack forbids a guessed formula, so the engine states the filed rate and no amount. Change: `src/AeroTech.Ancillary.Shopping/Engine/PriceAssessor.cs` (three expressions) and one constant in `Results/ShoppingReasonCodes.cs`. Readiness, eligibility and every other pricing unit are untouched.

Red run before the change: `P32B_ENG_07` failed (`IsOrderLevelTotalComplete = True`, no reason code). Green after it. No existing test had to change.

Second correction, found by the authenticated Backoffice run: a selection of quantity 99 was answered `Rejected` **with** `RequestedQuantityTotal` = 99 x unit (539,550,000 IRR on the live catalog). An amount on a selection the engine did not accept is not a trusted amount. `AncillaryShoppingEngine.EvaluateSelectionAsync` now returns the amount only when the status is `Accepted`; a rejected or incomplete selection keeps its unit rate and has no amount. Red first: `P32B_ENG_09` failed with `(Rejected, 5450, 539550)`. Green after it; no existing test had to change.

## 4. Source gaps that the engine reports instead of guessing

| Gap | Effect |
|---|---|
| chargeable weight of a per-kilogram product undefined | rate only, reason `ChargeableWeightNotDetermined` (Owner decision needed for any payable amount) |
| the producer states baggage pieces **and** weight on every coupon and no allowance concept | the engine reads a piece allowance; a weight-concept product (A02) is never applicable from this source (`P32B_DET_04`); the derivation rule is the open question R1 already listed |
| fees per Ticket / OneWay / RoundTrip / SectorOrPortion | need an order-level quote; listed, never multiplied |
| airport IANA time zone, numeric fare family id, date of birth, usage ledger, existing services, seat characteristics | the rules that need them are `NeedsVerification`; other products stay discoverable |
| supplier / FlightFlow availability | "check still to be made"; never a guarantee |
