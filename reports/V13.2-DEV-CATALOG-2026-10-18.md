## 2. Rules used (Owner rulings of 2026-10-18)

**Fare families.** These are the AirPrice staging fare families (15 rows):

| Cabin | Basic | Basic Plus | Green | Flex |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economy | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Premium Economy | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| Business | 9 | 10 | – | 12 |
| First | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 |

**Benchmark mapping:**

| Our family | Qatar | Emirates | Lufthansa |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Lite | Special | Light |
| Basic Plus | Classic | Saver | Classic / Comfort |
| Green | Convenience | Flex | – |
| Flex | Comfort | Flex Plus | Flex |

Each product has one provision per fare-family group, so the first matching sequence decides whether the service is included (Free), sold (Paid), or not sold (NotAvailable). Examples:
- Basic has no checked bag, so its first bag is sold at a lower first-bag price (like LH Light).
- Economy Flex and all premium cabins get priority boarding free (like LH Flex).
- Basic cannot be upgraded (like EK Special).
- Business and First get lounge, fast track, overweight, golf/ski and Wi-Fi free.
- Premium cabins do not buy à la carte meals.

**Other rules:**
- **Currency.** Every non-Seat price is in IRR. EUR/USD benchmark amounts were converted at USD 1,050,000 and EUR 1,150,000 and rounded to 1,000 rials. Domestic prices are set directly in IRR, with 10% VAT as a tax component; international prices carry no VAT (my reading of the zero rate on international transport, not verified).
- **Markets.** Three zones as geography route pairs on our own airports:
  - domestic: THR–MHD/KER/KIH/GSM and MHD–KIH;
  - regional: IKA–IST/SAW/NJF/DXB;
  - long-haul: IKA–JFK.

  Lounges and fast track use the departure airport; CIP and meet & assist use the service airport.
- **Points of sale.** None (every provision sells everywhere). One exception: the Istanbul lounge has an extra travel-agency rate.
- **Inventory.**
  - Unlimited: baggage, special meals, pets, assistance, priority.
  - Supplier (each with its own external partner): upgrades (revenue desk), paid meals (caterer), medical (medical desk), lounges, fast track, CIP, Wi-Fi.
  - FlightFlow is not used, because its delegation reference is not connected.
- **Date basis.** Every product is dated by flight departure, including lounge, fast track and CIP, so fare-family rules are allowed on them.
- **Cancellation.** Every refundable paid provision carries filed cancellation rules (code `DOTAIR-ANCILLARY-TERMS-2026`):
  - free at 24 h or more before departure;
  - 50% of the service total within the last 24 h (waiver possible);
  - free when involuntary or when the supplier fails;
  - manual review for a no-show.

  Non-refundable products have no rules.

## 3. Evidence basis and what is not verified

**Verified on the airlines' own sites:**
- [Emirates checked baggage](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/before-you-fly/baggage/checked-baggage/): Economy Special/Saver/Flex/Flex Plus = 20/25/30/35 kg.
- [Emirates seat selection](https://www.emirates.com/us/english/book/about-booking-online/emirates-seat-selection/): seat selection free from Flex.
- [Emirates fare differences](https://emirates.com/pk/English/help/faqs/what-are-the-differences-between-special-saver-flex-and-flex-plus-fares): Special not upgradable.
- [Lufthansa Group European fares](https://business.lufthansagroup.com/pt/en/travel-solutions/our-european-fares): Light has no checked bag; Flex includes a seat and Priority Boarding.
- [Lufthansa Business and Premium Economy Light](https://business.lufthansagroup.com/th/en/news/new-light-fare-in-business-class-and-premium-economy-class).
- [Lufthansa pets in cabin](https://www.lufthansa.com/cd/en/travelling-with-animals): up to 8 kg including the container.
- [Lufthansa unaccompanied minors](https://www.lufthansa.com/by/en/unaccompanied-minors): fee per flight segment.

**From secondary or dated sources:** Qatar Lite/Classic/Convenience/Comfort = 20/25/30/35 kg and free preferred seat only on Comfort. Qatar's own trade page answered 403.

**Not verified — all amounts are mine.** They are in the range of the dated figures found (for example LH pet in cabin about EUR 35–50, LH intercontinental UMNR about EUR 100, Emirates Dubai lounge USD 100–175), but they are not current price lists. This covers:
- extra bag, weight package, overweight, oversize, equipment;
- pets, UMNR, lounges, CIP, fast track;
- priority, Wi-Fi, à la carte meals, upgrades.

**Not taken from AirPrice.** The staging fare families carry flags that contradict the benchmark (Economy Basic has `isSeatSelectionFree` and `hasLoungeAccess` true; Business has `hasLoungeAccess` false). The catalog follows the benchmark.

## 4. Incidents

- My first wipe script did not delete inventory policies. Its last statement hit a foreign key, and SQL Server kept the statements that had already run. The trial product `BAG_EXTRA_23` therefore lost its provisions and its read-model row, and the next run added a Draft duplicate.
- Repaired: both copies were removed and the product was entered again (8/8 provisions, policy Active). The wipe script now deletes policies and runs with `XACT_ABORT ON`.
