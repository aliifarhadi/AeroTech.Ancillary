## 6. Sales date and clock (PARITY-BO-04, no code change)

The request keeps AirAvail's meaning: `salesDate` is the commercial sale instant, the clock only its default. In Ancillary that instant becomes `AncillaryShoppingContext.EvaluatedAtUtc`, which the engine reads in seven places:

| Use | Place | Which instant it needs |
|---|---|---|
| provision and definition sales window | `ProvisionEvaluation.SharedRules.cs:35`, `:62` | commercial sales date |
| advance purchase | `ProvisionEvaluation.SharedRules.cs:286` | commercial sales date |
| meal catering lead time, airport-service lead time | `ProvisionEvaluation.ProfileRules.cs:233`, `:302` | commercial sales date |
| expiry of the source (`ValidUntilUtc <= EvaluatedAtUtc`) | `AncillaryShoppingEngine.cs:208` | trusted clock |
| inventory slots still open (`EndUtc > evaluatedAtUtc`) | `Reading/Sql/SqlInventoryConfigurationReader.cs:61` | trusted clock |
| `evaluatedAtUtc` of the result and of the response | `AncillaryShoppingEngine.cs:113` | trusted clock by its name; today it is the sales date |

On this route the flight offer has no validity limit (`ValidUntilUtc` is null), so the expiry check cannot fire; the slot filter and the response stamp do follow a caller-given date. Proposed narrow split, not built (engine contract, needs the Owner): add `SalesDateUtc` to the context for the four commercial rows and let `EvaluatedAtUtc` always be the server clock; the request stays as it is; `PARITY_BO_04` and the existing sales-window tests then move to the new member unchanged in meaning.
