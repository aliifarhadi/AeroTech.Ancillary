### 4.5 Read side

## 6. Consequences for existing v12.1 behaviour

- A definition without `Profile` / `VariantCode` / specification can still be stored and read, but it can no longer be activated, and no provision of it can be published.
- Products that belong to none of the nine profiles (travel insurance, eSIM, hotel room, transfers) can therefore not be published under v12.2. Their v12.1 tests will be converted to that rule and listed one by one in the final report; they are not deleted silently.
- Every published provision of a classified definition needs exactly one point of sale and a price origin that agrees with its outcome.
- New Tax components need a tax treatment.
