### 2.4 Cancellation rules inside the provision

- Contract: optional trailing member `ProvisionSettlementInput.CancellationRules` (`IReadOnlyList<ProvisionCancellationRuleInput>?`). No new route, use case or aggregate; it travels in every provision Define/Change body (all nine facades and the generic route), because `Settlement` is shared.
- Semantics: Create null/omitted/`[]` = no rules. Change null/omitted = keep the stored rules; `[]` = clear; a list = replace (an unchanged rule keeps its row id). Only a draft changes (existing rule).
- Read-back: the provision form (typed and generic) returns the rules under `settlement.cancellationRules`; when there are none the member is omitted, so the settlement keeps its four original members (`[JsonIgnore(WhenWritingNull)]` on the input). `GET Backoffice/v1/AncillaryProvisions/{id}` returns `cancellationRules` as a list (empty when none).
- Fields exactly as chapter 13.2: `RuleRef`, `ReasonKind`, `BeforeStartMinutesFrom`, `BeforeStartMinutesTo`, `PenaltyKind`, `PenaltyFixedAmount`, `PenaltyPercentage`, `PenaltyCurrencyId`, `PercentageBase`, `WaiverPermitted`, `SourceReference`. New enums in `Contracts/AeroTech.Messages/Ancillary/Enums`: `PenaltyKind` (None=1…ManualReview=5), `CancellationReasonKind` (Voluntary=1…NoShow=4), `CancellationPercentageBase` (ServiceBase=1, ServiceTotal=2). No equivalent enum existed.
- Domain guards at Define and Change (code 422/16302 `ProvisionIsInvalid`, field named as `ProvisionCancellationRule.<Field>`):
  - kind-exclusive fields: `FixedAmount` needs amount ≥ 0 and a known currency, and the amount may not be finer than that currency's decimal places (same check as pricing); `Percentage` needs 0 < p ≤ 100 (at most 4 decimals) and a base; every other kind carries neither;
  - `SourceReference` required for `None`, `FixedAmount` and `Percentage`; optional for `ProviderQuoted` and `ManualReview`;
  - window: From ≥ 0 inclusive, To > 0 exclusive, From < To; rules of the same reason may not overlap (missing From = 0, missing To = open);
  - `RuleRef` required, at most 50 characters, no surrounding spaces, unique within the provision.
- Publish without rules is allowed and creates no penalty or refund meaning (S3 will quote `ProviderQuoted` or `NeedsManualReview`).
- Storage: child tables `Ancillary.ProvisionCancellationRules` (FK cascade, unique `(AncillaryProvisionId, RuleRef)`) and `ReadModel.AncillaryProvisionCancellationRules`; amount `decimal(19,6)`, percentage `decimal(9,4)`, `RuleRef` 50, `SourceReference` 256. Migrations `V132ProvisionCancellationRules` and `V132ProvisionCancellationRulesQuery` (create table and index only) were applied to the dev DB `DotAirAncillary`; ReferenceData was already up to date. No data was wiped or changed.
